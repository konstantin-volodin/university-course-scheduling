Telfer Instance

What the solver is given before it decides anything: the week it may schedule into, the people available to teach, the sections that must be covered, and the rooms they can sit in. Read together, these say what the answer can look like — and where it is forced.

Supply against demand

Every named professor carries a teaching load. Add those up and compare with the sections needing cover: the gap is what has to be bought in as part-time teaching, and it is fixed by these inputs before the solver places a single class. No schedule, however good, can close it.

The week on offer

Timeslots are fixed patterns, not free hours. Each one names the days it meets on and whether it is a single or a double block — a dashed garnet border marks a double. A section assigned to a two-day slot meets on both days.

Where the demand sits

Sections to cover, split by semester and by time of day. Night is the single largest block of demand, which matters because night teaching needs a professor who has agreed to it — see the permissions below for whether that binds in this instance.

Who can teach, and what they will accept

Teaching loads across the faculty, and the permissions that narrow who can take a given section.

Rooms

Six rooms, and the seats in each. Anything that does not fit — or that a part-timer teaches — goes off campus, which the model always allows.

Where the part-timers come from

The model injects one reserved professor id, part-timer, that is exempt from the no-overlap rules, so any number of sections can pile onto it. A penalty keeps that count down, but nothing forbids it — which is why a shortfall in supply shows up as part-time hiring rather than as an infeasible model.

Qualification is a filter, not a preference

100% of all professor–course pairs are marked qualified — a pair that is not qualified is not a disliked assignment, it cannot be made at all. Here it filters nothing at all: every professor is marked qualified for every course, so the constraint is inert on this instance.

Preferences only decide the last stage

Course, time and block preferences carry no weight until teaching load, concurrency and off-campus have each been settled in turn. They break ties inside an answer that is already fixed on the things that matter more.
